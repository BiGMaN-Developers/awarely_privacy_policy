1. Data Collection and Usage

a. Local Processing and Cloud Services

Local Processing: Many of Awarely's features, preferences, settings, and app operations are processed or stored locally on your device.

Cloud Services: Certain functionality relies on Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Functions. These services may process technical identifiers and application-related information required to provide app functionality.

AI Processing: When you use AI-powered features, text or reasons you enter may be transmitted to an external AI service provider to generate a response. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into these features.

b. Personal Data and Account Information

Anonymous Authentication: The App may create an anonymous Firebase Authentication account to support app functionality. This account is associated with a unique Firebase user identifier (UID).

Optional Google Sign-In: Users may optionally sign in using Google. If they choose this option, account information such as their name and email address may be processed through Google and Firebase Authentication. Certain account information may also be stored locally on the device.

Device and Application Identifiers: The App may process Firebase user IDs (UIDs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens. FCM tokens and associated identifiers may be stored in Cloud Firestore to support notifications and app functionality.

c. Analytics and Diagnostics

No Analytics SDKs: Awarely does not currently integrate Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics.

Firebase Infrastructure: Although analytics SDKs are not used, the App uses Firebase services including Authentication, App Check, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions for operational functionality. The use of these services should not be interpreted as a claim that no information is transmitted to third parties.

Diagnostics: Additional diagnostic information may be collected by the operating system or app distribution platform according to their respective privacy policies.

No Automatic Server Deletion: Uninstalling the App or clearing its local data does not automatically delete information stored in Firebase or other third-party services.

Data Deletion Requests: To ask about information associated with your use of Awarely or available deletion options, contact us at bigmandevelopers@gmail.com. A request may require verification and will be handled subject to applicable law and technical capabilities.
